Every viewer who writes gets a voice and keeps it. The **Viewers** page lists them per profile, with their voice, their last message and how many messages they wrote.

![The Viewers page](viewers.webp)

## Change a voice {#change}

Choose another voice in the **Voice** column. The viewer speaks with it from their next message on, in every language. With the voice mode *preset*, you choose among the preset's voices by name – listen to them first on the [Presets](presets#listen) page.

- **New random voice** – the viewer gets another one with the next message.
- **Effect** – one viewer can get an effect of their own (for example *robot* for your favorite bot-like regular), or none, whatever their voice normally has.
- **Plugin effect** – with [plugin effects for viewers](effects#viewer-plugins) set up: *at random* (what the profile's share gives them), none, or a particular one.
- **Mute** – the viewer is no longer read in this profile. **Read again** undoes it.
- **Forget this viewer** – as if they never wrote: a new voice with the next message.

A voice pinned in the profile under **Fixed voices for viewers** is marked here and can only be changed there.

## How voices are chosen {#how}

With the voice mode *preset*, a new viewer gets one of the preset's voices, chosen from their Twitch ID – so the same viewer lands on the same voice again, even on another PC. With many viewers some share a voice; using more of the preset's voices makes that rarer. If a viewer's voice isn't computed in a language yet, another computed voice of the same preset stands in until it is. The choice is saved with the profile; it survives restarts and updates.

Only viewers who actually wrote appear. The page shows the most recent ones; search by name to find others.
