The **Voices** page lists all voices in Voice Creator Pro: the ones TTV-TTS computed for presets, your own voices and anything else you made there.

![The Voices page](voices.webp)

## Try a voice {#try}

Type a text, choose a voice, a language, optionally an **emotion** and an **effect**, and click **Speak**. TTV-TTS reports how long it took until the first sound, how long the audio is and how fast it was generated (for example *0.5× real time*: one second of speech took half a second).

This is the quickest way to hear how a voice handles a name, an emote or a language before a stream.

## Ready or not {#primed}

Voice Creator Pro prepares a voice the first time it speaks (this takes a few seconds once). Voices marked **ready** are prepared; **prepares on first use** means the first message with that voice takes a little longer. Voices TTV-TTS computes for presets are prepared right away.

## Where voices come from {#sources}

- **Preset voices** – designed from a preset's descriptions, one per language. Their names are the characters' names (for example *Borin Ashbeard*). Manage them on the [Presets](presets) page.
- **Imported voices** – from a [voice pack](packs) or a ready-made download.
- **Your own** – anything you designed or cloned in Voice Creator Pro. Use them with the voice modes *voice per viewer*, *voice per language* or *one voice*, or pin them to viewers in the [profile](profiles#voices).

If you delete a preset voice in Voice Creator Pro, **Presets → Check voices** notices it and removes it from TTV-TTS's library; **Compute** makes it again.
