Computing a preset takes time on your graphics card. A **voice pack** (`.ttvpack`) carries a preset together with its computed voices, so whoever imports it doesn't have to compute anything.

## Download ready-made voices {#download}

Many built-in presets can be downloaded ready-made: click **Get** on a preset. TTV-TTS shows how many voices it would add per language and how many you have already. The voices were computed with Voice Creator Pro by TTV-TTS, with the [quality check](presets#quality); they are downloaded from GitHub and saved in your Voice Creator Pro – a few megabytes and about a minute, instead of hours.

## Share voices {#export}

On a preset's **Voices** dialog, **Share voices** saves a pack with the preset and the lossless samples of its computed voices. **Select** lets you pick single voices in one language to share just those. TTV-TTS shows the size before saving.

Send the file to a friend – by chat, cloud drive or USB stick.

## Import a pack {#import}

**Presets → Import pack** opens a `.ttvpack` file and shows what's inside: the voices per language (*you have it*, *will be added*, *added as a copy*) and the presets. **Add** saves the voices in Voice Creator Pro one by one, in pauses between chat messages – you can stream meanwhile. **Add the presets** adds the pack's presets, so the voices show up with their names and descriptions.

Voices you already have are skipped, so importing the same pack twice costs nothing.

> [!NOTE]
> A pack contains the preset and the voices' audio samples – nothing else from your PC. Voice Creator Pro rebuilds each voice from its sample, so imported voices sound like the originals.
